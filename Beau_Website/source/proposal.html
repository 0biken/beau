From finding a service
to keeping an appointment.

Beau will connect customers with verified beauty and wellness businesses nearby, bringing discovery, scheduling, communication and payments into one web experience.

Customers need more than a directory. They need to understand what a business offers, where the appointment will happen, what it costs and whether the time is genuinely available. Businesses need a dependable way to present their services, organize their teams and turn customer interest into paid appointments.

Our proposal is to design and build a responsive marketplace for Nigeria, with separate customer, business and administration portals. The service catalogue will support hairstylists, barbers, spas, massage providers, manicure and pedicure specialists, and perfumery-related consultations. Beau’s own service transactions will be priced and collected in Nigerian naira.

The first release will focus on bookable services. A responsive website makes these journeys accessible on phones, tablets and computers while establishing the operational foundation for future expansion.

Trust is part of the product.

Verification, clear portfolio labels, monitored conversations and booking-linked reviews will support informed decisions. Reliable scheduling and traceable payments will support the appointment itself.

The proposed build follows the agreed Beau PRD. Operating policies and commercial settings still awaiting approval will remain visible decisions rather than hidden assumptions.

The proposed solution

One marketplace.
Three connected workspaces.

Each portal serves a distinct role. They share the same booking records and business rules, with permissions that keep private information and operational actions in the right hands.

Customer portal

Discover with confidence.
Book with clarity.

Customers will search nearby businesses, compare services and understand the price, location and available time before paying a deposit.

  • Registration, verification and account management.
  • Location search, filters, business pages and labeled portfolios.
  • Live appointment selection, deposit checkout and balance payment.
  • Text enquiries, booking changes, reminders and dispute support.
  • Public, booking-linked ratings and reviews.

Business portal

A practical operating workspace.

Owners and authorized staff will manage services, portfolio images, branches, availability, appointments and customer enquiries. Subscription controls and finance summaries will make business activity understandable.

Admin portal

Oversight with an audit trail.

Authorized administrators will review verification, moderate content and conversations, resolve cases, configure policies and reconcile payments. Sensitive access and financial actions will be permission-controlled and recorded.

What the launch will include.

The following capabilities form the agreed service-marketplace scope. Detailed behavior and acceptance scenarios remain documented in the Beau PRD.

Verification before action

Anyone can register and browse. Businesses can prepare private drafts. Booking, payments, messaging, reviews and public publishing require completed verification, combining API checks with protected document submission and manual review. Pending, rejected and resubmitted applications will have clear status and next steps.

Nearby service discovery

Customers can permit location access or select an area manually, then filter by category, service price, distance, rating and availability. Results will lead to the relevant business branch, with service descriptions, duration, pricing, portfolio labels and public reviews.

Scheduling that respects capacity

Branch hours, staff availability, breaks, service duration and buffers will determine bookable slots. Temporary payment reservations and server-side conflict checks will prevent overlapping appointments, including when one professional works across branches. Changes and cancellations will retain their history.

Deposits, balances and commission

An admin-configured percentage deposit will confirm an available appointment after payment verification. The balance will be paid through Beau before service begins. Commission will be calculated once against the full service price, with separate records for fees, refunds and vendor entitlements.

Branches and subscription tiers

Basic, Growth and Business plans will provide progressively broader branch, staff and reporting capabilities. Owners will manage permitted branches and roles, with clear upgrade, downgrade and renewal behavior. Exact prices and numerical limits require approval; existing paid appointments must survive plan restrictions.

Text conversations within Beau

Verified customers and vendors can exchange text, including pre-booking enquiries. Direct contact details will be hidden and obvious contact-sharing blocked. Messaging is not end-to-end encrypted: the interface will disclose that authorized administrators can view conversations for support, safety and disputes, with audited access.

Honest portfolio images

External uploads will display Not captured in Beau. The in-site camera will support capture, preview and replacement, with labeled upload fallback when camera access fails. A fresh camera asset will have its own source record; the original external label will not be removed. Camera capture does not prove authorship.

Automatic image quality cleanup

Camera images will receive conservative exposure, colour, orientation, framing, resizing and compression improvements. Vendors can preview and revert enhancements. Processing must preserve the actual hairstyle, treatment or service outcome. Generative restyling and misleading retouching are outside this proposal.

Public reviews from both sides

Customers can review businesses and businesses can review customers after eligible completed bookings. Reviews will be linked to the appointment, with reporting, replies and moderation. Public customer identity will be minimized. A blind-publication window is a proposed operating policy requiring approval.

Notifications and financial oversight

In-platform notices and email will cover verification, bookings, balance payments and account events, with SMS for approved critical events. Delivery tracking, retries and duplicate prevention will support reliability. Admin tools will track disputes, refunds, reconciliation and completion-based payout eligibility, subject to provider capability.

Policies remain explicit.Deposit and commission percentages, subscription limits, cancellation charges, verification documents, review windows and payout timing must be agreed before their dependent production functions are enabled. Proposed defaults in the PRD are not approved commercial values.

How people will use Beau.

The customer journey

  1. Find the right service

    Choose a location, explore nearby providers and compare the service, price, branch, reviews and image-source labels.

  2. Verify and reserve

    Complete required verification, select live availability and review the full cost, deposit, balance and appointment terms.

  3. Pay and prepare

    Pay the deposit to confirm. Receive attendance details, communicate in Beau and pay the balance before the service starts.

  4. Attend and review

    Complete the appointment, leave an eligible review and access support if the booking or payment needs resolution.

The business journey

  1. Register and prepare

    Submit owner and business verification while creating private branch, service and schedule drafts.

  2. Activate the business

    Receive approval, choose an approved subscription, assign verified staff and publish eligible services and portfolios.

  3. Manage appointments

    Respond to enquiries, maintain calendars, receive booking notifications and record attendance and completion.

  4. Understand performance

    Review branch activity, customer feedback, commission and payout records within the selected plan’s permissions.

Failed payments, expired slots, camera permission denial and verification change requests will have recovery paths. A pending provider response will never be presented as confirmed payment, and a failed reschedule will preserve the original appointment.

Review the whole journey.

A successful screen is only one part of acceptance. A customer must be able to move from a verified account to a genuine available slot, a verified deposit, a fully paid appointment and a review. A business must be able to deliver that service and understand the related financial record. We will test those connections together, including delayed provider responses and failed actions.

The staging environment will support structured client review before production use. Feedback will be recorded against the agreed scope, with defects distinguished from requested additions. Changes affecting cost, integrations or delivery timing will be documented for agreement before they become commitments.

Handover should be usable.

The handover will identify where the application is hosted, who controls the relevant accounts, how privileged access is managed and how operational issues are escalated. Backup and recovery arrangements will be reviewed alongside the booking and payment flows.

Final source-code ownership, account ownership, access-transfer details and acceptance dates will be recorded in the delivery agreement. No proprietary rights or indefinite operating responsibility are assumed by this proposal.

What maintenance is intended to cover

The proposed service covers investigation and correction of defects in the delivered functionality, routine application checks and small upkeep tasks agreed within the maintenance scope. Reported issues will need a clear description and, where possible, reproduction steps. Priority, turnaround and escalation rules will be agreed rather than implied by the monthly price.

The free month is a defined transition after launch and handover, not an unlimited pool of future engineering work. The paid service follows that period at the stated monthly rate, with its commencement and invoicing recorded in the agreement.

What requires separate agreement

New modules, major redesigns, expanded integrations, substantial data migrations and changes to the agreed marketplace model are additional work. Third-party subscriptions, transaction charges, SMS usage, hosting and storage remain distinct from the maintenance fee. Provider outages may require coordination but cannot carry a guaranteed recovery time from KARMA without an agreed support arrangement.

Infrastructure coverage will be itemized before purchase. Any allowance shortfall, renewal or usage-based expense will be discussed explicitly, with responsibility for payment and account access confirmed in advance.

What we will agree
before commencement.

Scope and operating rules

Confirm the detailed launch scope and approve deposit/commission rates, tier prices and limits, cancellation/refund rules, verification requirements, review policies and payout conditions. Existing booking terms must remain traceable after configuration changes.

Providers and access

Confirm payment-provider support for the collection and payout arrangement, verification APIs, notification services, hosting and media storage. The client supplies required business accounts, approvals, content and access. Usage costs and renewal responsibilities remain subject to agreement.

Quality and acceptance

Acceptance will cover core journeys, role isolation, booking conflicts, duplicate payment callbacks, media labels, monitored message access and accurate financial records. Critical defects must be resolved before release; the client approves UAT and the production go/no-go.

Future releases

Product checkout and fulfilment, AI booking assistance, native mobile apps, generative image editing and multi-country operations are outside launch. Perfumery is a supported service category, not a commitment to retail checkout. Additional scope requires a written change agreement.
